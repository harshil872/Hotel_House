Our Story

A decade of passion, craftsmanship, and an unwavering love for the culinary arts.

How It All Began

Born From a Passion
for Perfection

In 2010, Chef Marco Delacroix returned from a decade of training across the finest kitchens of Lyon, Tokyo, and New York with a singular vision: to create a dining experience that felt both luxurious and deeply personal.

Savoir opened its doors in the heart of New York's Gourmet District with just twelve tables. From the very first evening, the city took notice. Within six months, reservations were booked three weeks in advance.

"Cooking is not just feeding the body. At its highest expression, it feeds the soul."
— Chef Marco Delacroix, Executive Chef & Founder

Today, Savoir boasts four dining rooms, a private wine cellar, and an events space — yet we've never lost the intimacy and care of those first twelve tables. Every guest is our most important guest.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Farm to Table

We source exclusively from sustainable local farms and ethical global producers. Freshness is non-negotiable.

Cooked with Love

Every dish is crafted with genuine passion. We don't cook for critics; we cook for you — our beloved guest.

Uncompromising Quality

From the finest Wagyu to the freshest herbs, quality is the foundation of everything we do, always.

Global Inspiration

Our menu draws from French, Japanese, and Mediterranean traditions, creating a language of flavor all our own.

Sustainability First

Zero food waste, composting, carbon-offset partnerships — because we love this planet as much as we love food.

Community

We employ locally, support artisan producers, and give back 2% of annual profits to food access programs.
