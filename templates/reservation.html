Table Reservation

Reserve your table for an exceptional culinary journey at Savoir.

Dining Hours

We welcome guests for lunch and dinner service 7 days a week.

  • Lunch: 12:00 PM – 2:30 PM
  • Dinner: 6:00 PM – 11:00 PM
  • Weekend Late Lounge until 12:00 AM

Dining Guidelines

  • Smart Casual Attire: We kindly request smart casual attire for all dinner services.
  • Grace Period: Tables will be held for 15 minutes past reservation time.
  • Large Parties (8+): Please contact our events team at events@savoir.dining.
  • Dietaries: Please inform us of any severe allergies when booking.
