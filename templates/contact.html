Frequently Asked Questions

Guest Assistance

What is the dress code?

We enforce a Smart Casual to Elegant dress code for dinner service. We kindly ask guests to refrain from wearing athletic wear, flip-flops, or beachwear. Jackets are recommended for men but not mandatory.

Is valet parking available?

Yes, complimentary valet parking is available at our main entrance on Gourmet Avenue every evening starting at 5:00 PM.

What is your corkage fee policy?

We welcome guests to bring special bottles from their personal cellar. Our corkage fee is $65 per 750ml bottle (up to 2 bottles per party), provided the wine is not currently featured on our reserve wine list.

Can we host private events or buyouts?

Absolutely. Our Private Dining Room accommodates up to 30 guests, and full venue buyouts are available for up to 120 guests. Please email events@savoir.dining to receive our events brochure.
